Kanal Rapor

Private, single-user tool used only by Umut Öztürk to manage his own YouTube channel Komedi Gözlüğü. It is not a public service and has no other users.

What it does

  • Uploads the owner's own original Shorts to his own channel (videos.insert) as private with a scheduled publishAt time (09:00, 15:00, 21:00 Turkey time), using titles written by the owner.
  • Reads the owner's own channel statistics (YouTube Data API v3, YouTube Analytics API) to measure video performance.

How it works

Runs as a command-line script on the owner's computer. Authorization is OAuth 2.0 with the owner's Google account only. No other channel's or user's data is accessed; nothing is shared with third parties; data stays on the owner's computer.
